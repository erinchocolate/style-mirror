## Naming conventions

- Lowercase + underscores for filenames.
- Dated files: `YYYYMMDD_<name>.md`. Sessions add a counter: `YYYYMMDD_NNN_<scenario>.md`.
- Rule ids: `ph_` (phrasing), `st_` (structure), `tn_` (tone), `lx_` (lexicon), prefixed scenario ids like `tc_` (team_chat). Session ids: `s_YYYYMMDD_NNN`.
